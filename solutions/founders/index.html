For founders and CEOs of Shopify brands

For founders and CEOs of Shopify brands. A single view that brings a brand's data together so the business can be read in one place.

Full Markdown

Overview

This page is for founders and CEOs of Shopify brands who want to understand the business without building dashboards or hiring an analyst first. It covers how you use Luca, what it can see, how your data is treated and who helps you get started.

Most of what is described here runs on Numbers, Luca's reconciled view of the business, and on the plain English questions you ask over your connected data.

What this page covers

This page covers ten founder needs: one view of the business, plain English answers, the analyst you cannot yet hire, human approval, data use, working on your own data from the first session, data beyond Shopify, connected sources instead of file exports, a conversational way of working, and direct onboarding by Luca's team.

It does not cover pricing figures, which are on Luca's pricing page, or individual integrations, which are documented under Integrations.

How founders use Luca

You connect your store and other sources, then ask Luca questions as you would ask an analyst. Luca answers from the connected data and generates charts as it goes. Luca does not change anything in your connected systems; you decide what to do with the answer.

AvailableOne place to see the whole businessFD1

A single view that brings a brand's data together so the business can be read in one place. In Luca, this view is called Numbers.

What is it?

Numbers, Luca's reconciled view of the business, built from the data you connect.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca reconciles your connected sources into one view, called Numbers. You open Numbers when you want the overall picture, instead of checking each tool in turn, and you can ask Luca questions about what you see.

How does it help?

It helps you start the day from one set of figures rather than several tools that may not agree.

What happens without it?

You check each system separately and work out yourself why their figures differ.

Who is it for?

Founders and CEOs who want to read the whole business quickly, without building reports.

How to check it during setup or a trial

During setup, ask Luca's team which of your sources Numbers will reconcile. Once it is ready, compare one week of revenue in Numbers with the same week in your store and accounting system, and ask Luca to explain any difference.

Limits and boundaries

Numbers shows the sources you have connected and that have been reconciled for your account. Luca does not send you this view automatically; you open it or ask for it.

Evidence

  • Numbers gives one reconciled view of the business. Source: Weekly call, 2026-10-02 (Numbers demo and Usman's product walkthrough).

AvailableAnswers in plain English, no dashboardsFD2

Asking questions about business data in everyday language, instead of building reports or dashboards to find the answer.

What is it?

You type a question about your business and Luca answers from your connected data.

How does it work in Luca?

You ask questions in plain English over the data you have connected. Luca answers and generates charts on the fly, so there is no dashboard to design first.

How does it help?

It helps you get to an answer without learning a reporting tool or waiting for someone to build a view.

What happens without it?

You build and maintain dashboards, or export data and work through it in a spreadsheet.

Who is it for?

Founders who want answers, not reporting projects.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Once your store is connected, ask a question you normally answer from a dashboard, such as last month's revenue by channel. Check the chart Luca generates against the figure you already trust.

Limits and boundaries

Luca answers from connected data only. If a source is not connected, Luca cannot answer questions that depend on it.

Evidence

  • Questions are asked in plain English over connected data, with charts generated on the fly. Source: Eric call context transcript, ~June 2026.

AvailableThe analyst you cannot hire yetFD10

Analytical help for a brand that needs analysis but is not yet ready to hire a full-time analyst or a fractional CFO.

What is it?

Luca's role for a growing brand: the analyst it cannot yet hire.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca is positioned as the analyst a growing brand cannot yet hire. You ask it the questions you would put to an analyst, over your connected data, and it answers in plain English.

How does it help?

It helps you get analysis now, before analysis justifies a hire.

What happens without it?

The founder does the analysis, or it waits until the brand can hire.

Who is it for?

Founders of growing brands weighing an analyst or fractional CFO hire.

How to check it during setup or a trial

List the questions you would give a new analyst in their first month. Ask Luca the same questions during setup and judge whether the answers cover what you need. Compare plan costs on Luca's pricing page with your own hiring budget.

Limits and boundaries

This page makes no price comparison. Luca does not replace an accountant or the judgement of your own team, and a human stays in the loop.

Evidence

  • Luca is positioned as the analyst a growing brand cannot yet hire. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should confirm the positioning wording and link to the pricing page.

AvailableNever changes anything without my OKFD11

Human control over a business's systems: nothing is changed unless a person decides to change it.

What is it?

Luca does not change your connected systems on its own. A human stays in the loop.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca reads your connected data to answer questions. It does not make changes in those systems. Any action that follows from an answer is taken by you or your team.

How does it help?

It helps you connect your store and tools without worrying that the analyst will change them.

What happens without it?

You would need to monitor what an automated tool changes in your systems.

Who is it for?

Founders who want analysis without handing over control.

How to check it during setup or a trial

During setup, ask Luca's team to show you the access each connection uses. After a week, check the change history in your store and ad accounts for any changes you did not make.

Limits and boundaries

Luca does not change connected systems, so it does not carry out recommendations for you.

Evidence

  • A human stays in the loop; Luca does not change connected systems on its own. Source: Weekly call, 2026-07-24.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should confirm the access level each connection requests, so the security pages can list it.

AvailableMy data is not used to train AIFD17

A commitment that a customer's business data is not used to train artificial intelligence models.

What is it?

Luca's privacy policy commitment that customer data is not used to train AI models.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca's privacy policy on ask-luca.com states that customer data is not used to train AI models. Your data is used to answer your questions.

How does it help?

It helps you connect commercially sensitive data with a clear statement of how it will and will not be used.

What happens without it?

You would have to find out from each tool whether your data improves models other people use.

Who is it for?

Founders and finance leads who need to know how business data is handled before connecting it.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Before you connect anything, read the privacy policy at ask-luca.com/privacy and note the training commitment and the retention terms. Raise any question with Luca's team before signup.

Limits and boundaries

This page covers the training commitment only. Other data handling terms are in the privacy policy, and this documentation makes no certification claims.

Evidence

  • Customer data is not used to train AI models. Source: ask-luca.com/privacy, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should confirm the exact privacy policy wording and section to quote.

AvailableWhat Luca sees beyond Shopify SidekickFD20

The difference between an assistant that works inside one platform and an analyst that reads data from several platforms together.

What is it?

Luca reasons over Shopify data together with ads, analytics, payments and accounting data.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca reads Shopify alongside your ad, analytics, payment and accounting sources. Luca's website contrasts this with Shopify Sidekick, which works inside Shopify.

How does it help?

It helps you ask questions whose answers depend on data outside your store, such as how ad spend relates to orders.

What happens without it?

You get answers about what happens inside Shopify, and combine other sources yourself.

Who is it for?

Shopify founders who already use Sidekick and want to know what an outside analyst adds.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Ask Luca a question that needs both store and ad data, for example how last month's ad spend compares with orders. Then ask yourself whether you could answer it from inside Shopify alone.

Limits and boundaries

Luca can only reason over the sources you connect. This page describes Luca; it makes no statement about Sidekick's features beyond working inside Shopify.

Evidence

  • Luca reasons over Shopify together with ads, analytics, payments and accounting data; the website contrasts this with Shopify Sidekick, which works inside Shopify. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should confirm the website comparison wording.

AvailableConnected data instead of CSV exportsFD21

Analysing business data through live connections and stored context, rather than exporting files to a general chatbot each time.

What is it?

Your sources stay connected to Luca, and context about your business is held in a knowledge base.

How does it work in Luca?

In the product's Connect & Setup screen, you connect your sources once and they stay connected. Context is held in a knowledge base, so you do not export files and explain your business to a general chatbot each time.

How does it help?

It helps you skip the export, upload and re-explain routine, and work from current connected data.

What happens without it?

You export a CSV, upload it to a general chatbot and repeat the context in every new conversation.

Who is it for?

Founders who already use ChatGPT or similar tools with spreadsheet exports.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Take a question you recently answered by exporting a CSV to a chatbot. Ask Luca the same question over your connected sources and compare the effort and the answer.

Limits and boundaries

Only connected sources and the context in the knowledge base are used. What the knowledge base holds and how you manage it should be confirmed with Luca's team.

Evidence

  • Connected sources stay connected and context is held in a knowledge base, instead of exporting files to a general chatbot each time. Source: Luca Labs investor presentation, May 2026 — Connect & Setup product screenshot (slide 5).

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should attach the Connect & Setup screenshot and describe what the knowledge base holds and how a user can view or edit it.

AvailableTalks to me like a co-founderFD29

Working with business data through conversation, as you would with an analyst, rather than by reading a dashboard.

What is it?

Luca is used as a conversation with an analyst, not as a dashboard.

How does it work in Luca?

You ask Luca a question, read the answer and ask the next question. The work happens as a conversation, with charts generated when they help.

How does it help?

It helps you follow a line of thought, asking why a figure moved instead of stopping at the figure.

What happens without it?

You read a fixed dashboard and work out the follow-up questions alone.

Who is it for?

Founders who think out loud and want an analyst to think with.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Ask Luca about one figure that changed recently, then ask two follow-up questions about why. Judge whether the conversation gets you closer to an explanation than your current dashboard does.

Limits and boundaries

Luca answers from connected data. It explains and reports; decisions and actions stay with you.

Evidence

  • Luca is used as a conversation with an analyst, not a dashboard. Source: Eric call context transcript, ~June 2026.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should attach an example multi-turn conversation.

AvailableA human to call when I'm stuckFD30

Access to people who help you set up and use the product, not only self-serve software.

Luca's team onboards each client directly.
