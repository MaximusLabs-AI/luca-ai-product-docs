Overview

Luca is an AI analyst for e-commerce brands. It reads the data you connect from your store, ad platforms, analytics, payment and accounting tools, and answers your questions about the business in plain English.

Luca works done-with-you. Luca's team onboards each client directly, and a human stays in the loop: Luca does not change your connected systems on its own. This documentation is for buyers evaluating Luca, and for anyone who needs an exact account of what the product does today.

How this documentation is organised

Products describes what Luca does, including Numbers, the reconciled view of your business. Integrations describes the data sources Luca connects to and, where it applies, which ones are set up by Luca's team.

Solutions describes the same capabilities from the point of view of a specific buyer, such as a founder, a finance lead or a subscription brand. Pricing explains how plans are published, including currencies and partner pricing.

Security explains how your data is handled, including the commitments in Luca's privacy policy. Onboarding and support explains how Luca's team sets you up and works with you. Coverage lists what this version documents and what is still waiting for confirmation.

What is documented here

This version documents only capabilities that MaximusLabs has confirmed from Luca's product, website and team. Where a capability has a limit, for example it is set up by Luca's team or confirmed per account, the page states that limit.

Anything not yet confirmed is left out of this version. It is listed for Luca's team to confirm, and it will be documented once it is. If you need a capability that is not described here, ask Luca directly rather than assuming it is available.
