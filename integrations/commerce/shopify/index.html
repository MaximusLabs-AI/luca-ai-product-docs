Shopify

Shopify. A connection that brings data from a Shopify store into Luca. It is one of the sources Luca recommends connecting first.

Full Markdown

Overview

This page explains how Shopify connects to Luca and what that connection supports. It is for brands that run their store on Shopify.

Shopify is one of the two sources Luca's website suggests connecting first. Other sources, such as advertising, analytics and finance, are added around it.

What this page covers

This page covers the Shopify connector as it appears in Luca's Connect & Setup screen, where the connection shows states including Connected, Completed and Syncing.

It also covers how Shopify data has been used in Numbers. For one live client, Shopify data was reconciled with subscription and accounting data into Numbers.

Before you connect

Confirm with Luca which Shopify data is read and which store permissions the connection requests. This page does not list Shopify data fields.

AvailableShopify connectorU1

A connection that brings data from a Shopify store into Luca. It is one of the sources Luca recommends connecting first.

What is it?

The Shopify connector links your Shopify store to Luca so Luca can use your store data when you ask questions and when your Numbers view is built.

How does it work in Luca?

In the product's Connect & Setup screen, Shopify appears as a connected source. The connection shows states including Connected, Completed and Syncing.

For a live client, Shopify data was reconciled with subscription and accounting data into Numbers.

How does it help?

It gives Luca your store data as a base. This helps you look at store activity next to the advertising, analytics and finance sources you add later.

What happens without it?

If you sell on Shopify and do not connect it, Luca has no store data to reason across, and Numbers cannot include it.

Who is it for?

Brands that run their store on Shopify, and the founders, operators and finance leads who need store data alongside other sources.

How to check it during setup or a trial

During setup, connect Shopify and watch its state in the Connect & Setup screen until it shows as connected. Then ask Luca a question you already know the answer to from your Shopify admin and compare the two.

Limits and boundaries

This connector covers Shopify store data only. This page does not list the Shopify data fields read or the store permissions requested; confirm these with Luca.

Evidence

  • Shopify appears as a connected source in Luca's Connect & Setup screen, with connection states Connected, Completed and Syncing. Source: Luca Labs investor presentation, May 2026 — Connect & Setup product screenshot (slide 5).
  • The website tells new users to start with Shopify or Amazon, then add advertising, analytics and finance sources. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.
  • Shopify data was reconciled with subscription and accounting data into Numbers for a live client. Source: Weekly call, 2026-10-02 (Numbers demo and Usman's product walkthrough).
