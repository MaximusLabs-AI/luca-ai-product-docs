Pricing model

Pricing model. Published pricing means plans and prices are shown publicly, so you can read them without talking to sales.

Full Markdown
Home / pricing

Overview

This page explains how Luca's pricing is structured: what you pay for, what does not change your price and how billing works. It is for founders and finance leads comparing Luca with other analytics tools or with hiring an analyst.

It describes the pricing model only. For current plans and figures, see ask-luca.com/pricing, and for what happens when you sign up, see Trial and signup.

What this page covers

This page covers the pricing model: seat-based pricing, usage, integration fees, annual billing and how to think about cost against hiring. It does not list plan names, prices, discount sizes or trial terms, because those change.

Plans and prices are published on ask-luca.com/pricing, and you can read them there without a sales call. Check that page before you budget.

The model at a glance

Luca prices by seat. Store revenue, order volume and ad spend do not set the price, so the plan price does not rise as your brand grows.

Every plan lists unlimited usage. The website states that custom connectors carry no integration fee, and annual billing is offered at a discount to monthly billing. The size of that discount is shown on ask-luca.com/pricing.

AvailablePriced by seatCO4

Seat-based pricing charges for the number of people who use a product.

What is it?

It is a pricing model where cost follows the number of users rather than the size of the business.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca prices by seat. Your store revenue, order volume and ad spend do not set the price.

Current seat prices are on ask-luca.com/pricing.

How does it help?

This helps you predict cost from your team size rather than from your trading figures.

What happens without it?

Where pricing is tied to revenue, orders or ad spend, your bill can change with your trading results, which makes budgeting harder.

Who is it for?

Brands that want software cost tied to team size.

How to check it during setup or a trial

List the people who will use Luca, then use the seat prices on ask-luca.com/pricing to work out your cost. Ask Luca's team how adding or removing a seat is billed.

Limits and boundaries

This describes how the price is calculated, not the price itself. Confirm what counts as a seat with Luca's team.

Evidence

  • Luca prices by seat, not by store revenue, orders or ad spend. Source: ask-luca.com/pricing, read 2026-10-16.

AvailablePrice does not rise with your revenueCO5

A pricing model in which growth in revenue or orders does not increase what you pay.

What is it?

It means your plan price stays the same as your sales grow.

How does it work in Luca?

Because Luca prices per seat, the plan price does not rise as your revenue or order volume grows.

What you pay follows your seats and plan, not your trading volume.

How does it help?

This helps you grow sales without your Luca cost rising with them.

What happens without it?

With revenue-linked pricing, a strong month or season could raise your bill.

Who is it for?

Growing brands planning for higher volume.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Work out your cost at today's revenue and at your growth target using the seat prices on ask-luca.com/pricing. The two figures should differ only if you plan to add seats or change plan.

Limits and boundaries

Your price can still change if you add seats or move plan. Check ask-luca.com/pricing for current figures.

Evidence

  • Because pricing is per seat, the plan price does not rise as the brand's revenue or order volume grows. Source: ask-luca.com/pricing, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Confirm whether any plan has limits, such as on stores or connections, that could require a change of plan.

AvailableUnlimited usage on every planCO6

Unlimited usage means a plan does not cap or meter how much you use the product.

What is it?

It is a plan term that removes usage allowances, such as credits or question limits.

How does it work in Luca?

Every plan on ask-luca.com/pricing lists unlimited usage.

Seats, not usage, are the basis of the price.

How does it help?

This helps your team ask as many questions as the work needs without tracking an allowance.

What happens without it?

With metered or credit-based usage, teams often ration questions to control cost.

Who is it for?

Teams that expect to use Luca often.

How to check it during setup or a trial

During setup or a trial, have each seat holder work through the questions they usually answer in a week. Then read the plan details on ask-luca.com/pricing to confirm that level of use does not change your cost.

Limits and boundaries

This reflects what the pricing page lists. Read the plan details there for any usage terms.

Evidence

  • Every plan lists unlimited usage. Source: ask-luca.com/pricing, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Confirm whether any fair-use terms apply to unlimited usage.

AvailableNo integration feesCO15

An integration fee is a charge for connecting a data source or building a custom connector.

What is it?

It is a commitment not to charge separately for connecting your systems.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca's website states that custom connectors carry no integration fee.

Luca's team handles custom connectors and integration mapping as part of onboarding.

How does it help?

This helps you connect systems that need a custom connector without a separate setup bill.

What happens without it?

Where vendors charge per integration, connecting less common systems can add cost before you see any value.

Who is it for?

Brands whose systems need a custom connector.

How to check it during setup or a trial

List the systems you use that may need a custom connector and raise them with Luca's team before onboarding. Ask them to confirm in writing that no integration fee applies to each one.

Limits and boundaries

This covers integration fees for custom connectors. It does not set a timeline for building them; ask Luca's team.

Evidence

  • The website states custom connectors carry no integration fee. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Confirm the website wording and any limits on which custom connectors Luca's team will build.

AvailableCompared with hiring an analystCO17

A way to judge a tool's cost against the cost of hiring a person to do similar work.

What is it?

It is a cost frame that compares software with the analysis work a team does by hand or would hire for.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca is positioned as doing the work of an analyst and advisor for a growing brand that cannot yet hire one.

You ask questions in plain English and Luca answers from your connected data and the reconciled view in Numbers.

How does it help?

This helps you weigh the cost of Luca against the analysis time your team spends today.

What happens without it?

Without this frame, you may compare Luca only with other software and overlook the time your team spends on analysis.

Who is it for?

Growing brands without an in-house analyst or advisor.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Write down the analysis questions your team answered by hand last month. Ask Luca the same questions during setup or a trial, and compare the answers and the time taken.

Limits and boundaries

This is how Luca is positioned, not a guaranteed saving. Judge it against your own team's workload.

Evidence

  • Luca is positioned as doing the work of an analyst and advisor for a growing brand that cannot yet hire one. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Confirm whether Luca wants to publish a cost comparison with hiring, and which sources it would use.

AvailableAnnual billing discountCO11

An annual billing discount is a lower effective price for paying for a year at a time instead of monthly.

What is it?

It is a billing option that rewards a yearly commitment with a lower price than monthly billing.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca offers annual billing at a discount to monthly billing.

The size of the discount and the plan prices are shown on ask-luca.com/pricing.

How does it help?

This helps you lower your cost if you are ready to commit for a year.

What happens without it?

Without an annual option, you would pay the monthly rate whatever your commitment.

Who is it for?

Brands ready to commit to Luca for a year.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Compare the monthly and annual figures on ask-luca.com/pricing for your seat count. Before you choose, ask Luca's team how cancellation and seat changes work on annual billing.

Limits and boundaries

This page does not state the discount. Check ask-luca.com/pricing for current terms.

Evidence

  • Annual billing is offered at a discount to monthly. Source: ask-luca.com/pricing, read 2026-10-16.
