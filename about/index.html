Overview

This page introduces the company behind Luca: its founder, its named team, the AI model provider it uses and a recent example of product progress. It is for buyers assessing Luca as a vendor.

Luca is an AI analyst agent for e-commerce brands. For how your data is handled, see the Security section.

What this page covers

This page covers founder background, the team named on ask-luca.com/about, the disclosed language model provider and recent product progress. It reflects the about page and privacy policy as read on 16 October 2026, plus information from Luca's team.

Ask Luca's team for anything else your vendor review needs.

Who builds Luca

Eric Bidinger is co-founder and CEO. The about page also names Abbas Khaku (COO), Ciske Smit (Head of Product), Kaan Simsek (Head of Data), and engineers Hassaan Mirza and Usman Jameel.

AvailableFounder backgroundT14

Founder background describes the experience of the people leading a company.

What is it?

It is published information about who leads the company and what they have done before.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca's co-founder and CEO is Eric Bidinger, an aerospace engineer with 17 years in private equity.

This is published on ask-luca.com/about.

How does it help?

This helps you judge the experience behind a product that handles your financial data.

What happens without it?

Without it, you would know less about who is accountable for the product.

Who is it for?

Buyers assessing vendor risk.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Read the about page before a call with Luca's team, and use the call to ask about the product's direction and how the team's background shapes it.

Limits and boundaries

This covers the CEO's background as published on the about page. Ask Luca's team about other leadership experience if your review needs it.

AvailableAI model provider disclosedR5

Model provider disclosure means a vendor names the company whose language model its product uses.

What is it?

It is a public statement of which language model provider processes your data.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca's privacy policy names OpenAI as its language-model sub-processor.

OpenAI processes data under enterprise terms that exclude training on it.

How does it help?

This helps you include the model provider in your vendor review and check it against your own policies.

What happens without it?

Without disclosure, you would not know which third party processes your data through the language model.

Who is it for?

Buyers and reviewers assessing vendor and data risk.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Check that OpenAI is named in the privacy policy, and confirm your organisation's policy allows that provider. Ask Luca's team any follow-up questions about the enterprise terms.

Limits and boundaries

This names the provider as listed in the privacy policy read on 16 October 2026. Check the live policy for changes.

Evidence

  • OpenAI is named as the language-model sub-processor. Source: ask-luca.com/privacy, read 2026-10-16.
