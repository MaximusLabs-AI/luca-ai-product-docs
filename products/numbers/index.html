Numbers: the reconciled control centre

A reconciled database combines data from several systems and resolves differences between them, so each figure has one agreed value.

Full Markdown
Home / products / numbers

Overview

Numbers is the reconciled control centre in Luca. It brings data from your sources together into one clean database and shows it as a unified view above the chat. This page is for operators and finance or growth leads who want to understand what Numbers shows and how it is set up.

Numbers is the data layer: the dashboard is the reconciled data itself, with no AI in it. Luca, the agent, sits on top of it and answers questions using that data.

What this page covers

This page covers the reconciled database, the control-centre view, net revenue, cohort analysis, active and lapsed customers, subscription skip data, reconciliation with accounting data, and the data connections shown on Numbers.

Several of these are delivered through reconciliation work by Luca's team, not created automatically when you connect a source. Each item below says where that applies.

How Numbers is set up

Reconciliation is a project led by Luca's team. For the first Numbers client, reconciling Shopify, Recharge and accounting data took about three weeks of work by Luca's team.

Luca's team is rolling Numbers out for several clients. The work needed for your account depends on your sources, so confirm the plan with Luca's team.

AvailableOne reconciled database across your sourcesU32

A reconciled database combines data from several systems and resolves differences between them, so each figure has one agreed value.

What is it?

The single, clean database behind Numbers, built from your sources.

How does it work in Luca?

Numbers reconciles your Shopify, subscription and accounting data into one clean database and shows it as a unified view. Luca's team is rolling Numbers out for several clients.

Reconciliation is work led by Luca's team, as described under Reconciled with accounting data.

How does it help?

It helps you work from one set of figures rather than reconciling differences between tools yourself.

What happens without it?

Each system reports its own version of the same figure, and teams spend time explaining the gaps.

Who is it for?

Brands whose figures differ between their store, subscription and accounting systems, and the finance and growth leads who reconcile them.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Pick one figure you already track, such as last month's revenue, and compare the value in Numbers with your store and accounting systems. Ask Luca's team to explain any difference.

Limits and boundaries

Numbers reconciles the sources included in your reconciliation, which Luca's team sets up. It is not created automatically when you connect a source.

AvailableUnified control-centre viewU189

A control-centre view is a single screen that shows the main figures you need to run a business.

What is it?

The Numbers dashboard, shown above the chat in Luca.

How does it work in Luca?

Numbers is a control centre to run the business, shown above the chat. The dashboard is the reconciled data itself, with no AI in it.

Luca, the agent, sits on top of it and answers questions using that data.

How does it help?

Because the dashboard shows reconciled data rather than AI output, it gives you a fixed reference to check Luca's answers against.

What happens without it?

Who is it for?

Founders and operators who want one place to see how the business is doing.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Open Numbers, note a figure, then ask Luca a question about it in the chat. Check that the answer is consistent with the figure shown.

Limits and boundaries

The dashboard displays reconciled data and does not generate AI insights by itself. Which figures appear depends on your reconciliation.

AvailableNet revenueU50

Net revenue is revenue after deductions such as discounts, refunds and returns.

What is it?

A revenue figure in Numbers that reflects deductions, not only gross sales.

How does it work in Luca?

Net revenue is part of the reconciled commerce view in Numbers. Because it comes from reconciled data, it reflects one agreed set of figures rather than a single tool's report.

How does it help?

It helps you judge performance on the revenue the business keeps, not only on what was sold.

What happens without it?

Teams may rely on gross figures from a single tool, which can overstate performance.

Who is it for?

Finance leads, founders and growth teams.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Compare net revenue in Numbers for a recent month with the figure your finance team reports. Ask Luca's team which deductions are included.

Limits and boundaries

The deductions included depend on how your data is reconciled; confirm the definition with Luca's team.

Evidence

  • Net revenue is part of the reconciled commerce view. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.

AvailableCohort analysisU67

Cohort analysis groups customers by when they started, such as their first order or subscription month, and tracks how each group behaves over time.

What is it?

A view in Numbers that shows how groups of customers behave over time.

How does it work in Luca?

Cohort visibility in Numbers was built for a live subscription client who previously had no visibility of their cohorts. It is part of the reconciled view, so cohorts are built from reconciled data.

How does it help?

It helps you see whether newer customers behave differently from earlier ones, which supports retention decisions.

What happens without it?

You see totals but cannot tell whether retention is improving or worsening for newer customers.

Who is it for?

Subscription and repeat-purchase brands, and their retention and finance leads.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Ask Luca's team to show cohorts for your account, then pick one cohort and check its size against your own records.

Limits and boundaries

Cohort visibility was built for a subscription client's reconciled data. Confirm how cohorts are defined for your data model.

Evidence

  • Cohort visibility was built for a live subscription client who previously had 'zero visibility on the cohorts'. Source: Weekly call, 2026-10-02 (Numbers demo and Usman's product walkthrough).

To confirm with Luca before publication: Confirm cohort definitions and availability for non-subscription brands, and attach a screenshot.

Available · limited scopeActive vs lapsed customersU69

Active and lapsed customer status separates customers who are still buying or subscribed from those who have stopped.

Scope: Built for a subscription client's reconciled data; confirm for your data model.

What is it?

A view in Numbers that tells active customers apart from inactive ones.

How does it work in Luca?

Before Numbers, a subscription client could not tell active from inactive customers. The reconciled view addressed this, and it was built for that client's reconciled data.

How does it help?

It helps you size your active base and see how many customers have lapsed.

What happens without it?

Active and lapsed customers are mixed together, so customer counts can mislead.

Who is it for?

Subscription brands and the teams responsible for retention.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Ask Luca's team how active and lapsed are defined for your data, then check the counts against a sample of customers you know.

Limits and boundaries

Built for a subscription client's reconciled data; confirm for your data model.

Evidence

  • The same client could not tell active from inactive customers before Numbers; the reconciled view addressed it. Source: Weekly call, 2026-10-02 (Numbers demo and Usman's product walkthrough).

Available · limited scopeSubscription skips, pauses and gapsU180

Subscription skip and pause data records when subscribers skip or pause deliveries, which affects revenue even when they have not cancelled.

Scope: Delivered through a reconciliation project by Luca's team.

What is it?

A rebuilt history of subscription events, such as skipped orders, drawn from the systems that hold them.

How does it work in Luca?

This is delivered through a reconciliation project by Luca's team. In one client's case, Luca rebuilt the historical subscription-skip data from several systems.

The rebuild found a revenue shortfall of $72,000 that the client had not known about.

How does it help?

It can reveal revenue lost to skips that does not show up in standard reporting.

What happens without it?

Skips and gaps can stay hidden across systems, so revenue shortfalls go unnoticed.

Who is it for?

Subscription brands whose subscription data sits across several systems.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Ask Luca's team to scope a reconciliation of your subscription history. Once it is complete, compare skip counts for one period with your subscription platform's records.

Limits and boundaries

Delivered through a reconciliation project by Luca's team. The client result described is one case, not an expected outcome; confirm whether pauses are covered for your data.

Evidence

  • Luca rebuilt a client's historical subscription-skip data from several systems and found a previously unknown $72,000 revenue shortfall. Source: Luca client newsletter, 2026-10-12.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Confirm the client has agreed to the example being cited, and confirm which subscription events, including pauses, are covered.
