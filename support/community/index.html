Community and podcast

Community and podcast. A customer community is a shared space where users and prospects exchange ideas about a product.

Full Markdown

Overview

This page describes the Luca community and the E-Commerce Leaders Series podcast. It is for clients and prospects who want to learn from other operators.

Both sit alongside the onboarding Luca's team runs with you.

What this page covers

This page covers where the community is hosted, who it is for and what members exchange, and who hosts the podcast. It does not describe membership rules or episode schedules. Check those in the community or with Luca's team.

AvailableLuca communityS10

A customer community is a shared space where users and prospects exchange ideas about a product.

What is it?

It is a place to learn from other people using the same product.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca runs a community for clients and prospects on Heartbeat. You can reach it at community.ask-luca.com or through the Heartbeat apps for iOS and Android.

Members exchange prompts, workflows and use cases.

How does it help?

This helps you see how other brands ask questions of Luca and adapt their workflows to your own.

What happens without it?

Without a community, you would learn only from your own use and from Luca's team.

Who is it for?

Luca clients and prospects.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Join the community during your evaluation and look for workflows from brands like yours. Try one shared prompt on your own data and compare the result with how you answer that question today.

Limits and boundaries

The community is for exchanging prompts, workflows and use cases. Ask Luca's team how to join and where to raise account issues.

Evidence

  • Luca runs a community for clients and prospects on Heartbeat (community.ask-luca.com, plus iOS and Android apps) to exchange prompts, workflows and use cases. Source: Eric's community invitation email, 2026-08-25.

AvailableE-Commerce Leaders Series podcastT18

An owned podcast is a show a company produces itself. Luca's features conversations with e-commerce operators.

What is it?

It is a podcast hosted by Luca's founder in which e-commerce operators talk about their work.

How does it work in Luca?

Eric Bidinger, Luca's co-founder and CEO, hosts the E-Commerce Leaders Series podcast with e-commerce operators.

It sits alongside the community as a way to hear from other operators.

How does it help?

This helps you understand the questions and problems Luca's founder discusses with operators.

What happens without it?

Without it, you would have fewer ways to judge the thinking behind the product.

Who is it for?

Founders and operators of e-commerce brands.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Listen to an episode with an operator whose brand is similar to yours. Note the questions they raise, then ask Luca the same questions about your own data during setup or a trial.

Limits and boundaries

The podcast is not product documentation or advice for your brand.

Evidence

  • Eric hosts the E-Commerce Leaders Series podcast with e-commerce operators. Source: Eric call context transcript, ~June 2026.
