For Mastercard Business Bonus merchants

For Mastercard Business Bonus merchants. Partner pricing is a separate price list offered to members of a partner programme.

Full Markdown

Overview

This page is for merchants who reach Luca through Mastercard Business Bonus. It covers the partner pricing page, eligibility, the discount, the Mastercard listing and what the partner plans include.

Partner pricing gives you the same Luca plans as standard pricing, with a discount set out on Luca's dedicated Mastercard Business Bonus pricing page.

What this page covers

This page covers five partner needs. It does not state a discount percentage or eligibility criteria: the discount is shown on the partner pricing page, and eligibility is confirmed by Luca.

For the plans themselves, see Pricing.

Capabilities on this page

AvailableMastercard Business Bonus partner pricingCO16

Partner pricing is a separate price list offered to members of a partner programme. For Luca, it is a dedicated pricing page for Mastercard Business Bonus merchants.

What is it?

A dedicated Mastercard Business Bonus pricing page on ask-luca.com, separate from standard pricing.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca publishes a dedicated pricing page for Mastercard Business Bonus. Luca is also listed on Mastercard's own Business Bonus page, which is how many merchants first find it.

Eligibility for partner pricing is confirmed by Luca. This page does not state a discount percentage.

How does it help?

If you are a Business Bonus merchant, it gives you a partner route into the same product, with its own pricing page to compare against standard pricing.

What happens without it?

You would evaluate Luca on standard pricing only.

Who is it for?

Merchants who use Mastercard Business Bonus and are considering Luca.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Open the Mastercard Business Bonus pricing page on ask-luca.com and the standard pricing page side by side. Ask Luca's team to confirm that your business is eligible before you choose a plan.

Limits and boundaries

This page does not state a discount percentage or eligibility rules. Eligibility is confirmed by Luca.

Evidence

  • Luca has a dedicated pricing page for Mastercard Business Bonus. Source: ask-luca.com/pricing, read 2026-10-16.
  • Luca is listed on Mastercard's Business Bonus page. Source: Mastercard Business Bonus listing shown by Eric, 2026-10-02.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should confirm the URL of the partner pricing page, the eligibility rules and the current discount wording for the pricing section.

AvailableSize of the discountMB2

How much the Mastercard Business Bonus partner discount reduces the price of Luca's plans.

What is it?

The partner discount on Luca's plans for Mastercard Business Bonus merchants.

How does it work in Luca?

The Mastercard Business Bonus pricing page offers a discount on every plan, on both monthly and annual billing. The discount is shown on that page; this documentation does not state a percentage.

How does it help?

It helps eligible merchants reduce the cost of whichever plan suits them.

What happens without it?

You pay standard pricing.

Who is it for?

Eligible Mastercard Business Bonus merchants.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Compare the plan you want on the partner pricing page and the standard pricing page, for both monthly and annual billing. Ask Luca's team to confirm eligibility before you rely on the partner price.

Limits and boundaries

No discount percentage is stated here. The discount applies to eligible merchants, and eligibility is confirmed by Luca.

Evidence

  • The partner page offers a discount on every plan, monthly and annual. Source: ask-luca.com/pricing, read 2026-10-16.

To confirm with Luca before publication: Luca's team should confirm the current discount wording on the partner page.

What is it?

Luca is listed on Mastercard's Business Bonus page.

Luca appears on Mastercard's Business Bonus page beside large consumer brands. Merchants can find Luca there and then go to Luca's partner pricing page.

How does it help?

It helps you find Luca through a programme you already use.

What happens without it?

Mastercard Business Bonus merchants.

The listing shows that Luca is part of the programme. It is not a statement by Mastercard about Luca's product, and this page makes no certification claims.

What is it?

The partner discount applies to the same plans as standard pricing.

It helps you know that partner pricing does not mean a cut-down product.

What happens without it?

You would need to check plan by plan what a partner version leaves out.

Who is it for?
