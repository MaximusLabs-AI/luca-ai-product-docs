Onboarding: done with you

Hands-on onboarding means the vendor's team does much of the setup work with you, rather than leaving you to configure the product alone.

Full Markdown
Home / support / onboarding

Overview

This page explains how onboarding works with Luca and what Luca's team does for you during setup. It is for founders and operators planning when to start.

Onboarding is where your connected systems are mapped and reconciled into Numbers, Luca's reconciled view of your figures.

What this page covers

This page covers the onboarding service: hands-on setup by Luca's team, custom connectors, integration mapping and reconciliation into Numbers. It does not set out onboarding timelines. Ask Luca's team for the current intake and what to expect for your systems.

Onboarding intake

Onboarding is hands-on work for Luca's team, so Luca manages how many new clients it onboards at a time.

If your start date matters, ask Luca's team which intake you would join before you sign up.

AvailableHands-on onboarding with Luca's teamS3

Hands-on onboarding means the vendor's team does much of the setup work with you, rather than leaving you to configure the product alone.

What is it?

It is an onboarding service where the vendor's people do significant setup work for each client.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca's team does significant onboarding work for each client.

Because of this, Luca manages how many new clients it onboards at a time, so each one gets that support.

How does it help?

This helps you start with your systems set up by people who know the product, without your team doing all the configuration.

What happens without it?

With self-serve setup only, your team would carry the mapping and configuration work itself.

Who is it for?

Brands without spare analyst or engineering time for setup.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Before signing up, ask Luca's team which intake you would join and what they need from you. During onboarding, note which tasks the team handled and which fell to you.

Limits and boundaries

Onboarding capacity is managed by intake, so your start date depends on availability. This page does not set timelines; ask Luca's team.

  • Luca manages how many new clients it onboards at a time so each one gets that support. Source: Weekly call, 2026-09-25.

AvailableData mapping done by Luca's teamS4

Data mapping is matching fields and records across systems so that figures from different sources line up.

What is it?

It is the work of making data from separate systems agree, so a combined view can be trusted.

How does it work in Luca?

Luca's team handles custom connectors and integration mapping for you.

The team also reconciles your systems into Numbers, Luca's reconciled view of your figures.

How does it help?

This helps you get a consistent view across your sales, advertising, payments and accounting data without your team doing the mapping.

What happens without it?

Without vendor mapping, your team would need to work out how each system's figures relate before relying on a combined view.

Who is it for?

Brands with several connected systems and no in-house data team.

How to check it during setup or a trial

Pick a figure you already reconcile by hand, such as one month's revenue across your store and your accounting system. Once Luca's team has completed the mapping, compare it with Numbers and raise any differences with them.

Limits and boundaries

Mapping is done by Luca's team as part of onboarding. This page does not describe self-serve mapping.

Evidence

  • Luca's team handles custom connectors and integration mapping. Source: ask-luca.com, read 2026-10-16.
  • The team reconciles a client's systems into Numbers. Source: Weekly call, 2026-10-02 (Numbers demo and Usman's product walkthrough).
