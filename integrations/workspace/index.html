Overview

This page covers the ways you can work with Luca from your workspace and give it extra context. It is for teams that want to ask Luca questions where they already work, or add documents Luca can use.

These options sit alongside your data connections. They change where you ask questions and what context Luca has, not which data sources are connected.

Options in this category

Slack: the 'Add Luca to Slack' option lets you send Luca a direct message or mention Luca in a channel.

Documents and files: the 'Upload company docs' and 'Manage Knowledge Base' options let you add documents as context.
